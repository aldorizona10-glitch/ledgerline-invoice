FAQ

Good questions.

Is my invoice data really private?

Yes. Ledgerline is a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser. There is no server and no account — nothing you type is ever sent anywhere. You can even use it with your Wi-Fi turned off.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the file in any modern browser, or host it on a free service like Netlify. It works on desktop and mobile.

How do payments work?

Ledgerline doesn't process payments — you paste your own Stripe Payment Link, PayPal.me, or bank link, and it becomes a clickable "Pay" button on the invoice. No payment keys or fees ever touch Ledgerline.

Is it really a one-time price?

Yes. Pay once, own it forever — no subscription, no per-invoice charges, no expiry.

Can I use my own logo and branding?

Absolutely. Upload your logo, choose an accent color, and switch between Invoice, Quote, Estimate, and Receipt formats.
